# Visual production plan — make-money-with-ai

## Article art direction

- Medium: cinematic dark SaaS editorial photography with real freelance-service artifacts and a restrained infographic layer.
- Materials: matte black desk surfaces, dark paper, smoked glass, product mockups, printed case sheets, a presentation preview, a short-video storyboard, and compact metal stationery.
- Background: deep teal-black `#011417`.
- Accent: exact Opten lime `#9CFB51`, plus white text and neutral graphite.
- Typography treatment: flat generated Bebas Neue, condensed uppercase display sans-serif with tall narrow letters; no extruded, embossed, beveled, engraved, or separate 3D letters.
- Line language: thin `#9CFB51` frames, connector lines, check marks, arrows, and numbered nodes with one consistent stroke weight.
- Light and grain: directional low-key studio light, crisp subject separation, controlled reflections, subtle cinematic grain.
- Recurring motif: one small lime check node connects the task, proof, and delivery in every frame.
- Information density: one large hero label plus 2-4 connected labels that explain a real decision. No disconnected nouns added for decoration.
- Card/grid frames: 1
- Card/grid note: Frame 2 is the only card/matrix composition.
- Style anchor: Frame 1 RU. Later frames inherit medium, palette, flat Bebas Neue, line weight, check-node motif, light, and grain, but not its layout, camera, or object composition.
- Old blog usage: older blog JPGs are not attached to the generator. The `ai-headshot` and `ai-ugc` files in `seo2/Reference/` are used only as inspected visual guidance. Every generation attaches only `seo2/Reference/bebas-neue-font-reference.png`.
- RU language rule: all visible explanatory text is Russian. English appears only in the EN-localized sibling.

## Cover — honest AI income workflow

- Story beat: income comes from a clear client result and review process, not from pressing an AI button.
- Visual proof: one cinematic service-planning scene with a central finished client delivery folder surrounded by a presentation preview, product-card mockup, short-video storyboard, client brief, and checked delivery note.
- Composition archetype: hero still life.
- Text mode: none.
- Semantic job: show several sellable outcomes without money piles, hype, dashboards, or readable copy.

## Frame 1 — УСЛУГА / SERVICE

- Story beat: a skill becomes sellable only after it is turned into a bounded service and one concrete result.
- Visual proof: a real creator at a dark workstation connects a rough working draft on the left to one polished client deliverable on the right; three linked checkpoints define the conversion.
- Composition: cinematic split-screen flow
- Text mode: sequence
- RU text: `УСЛУГА`, `НАВЫК`, `ГРАНИЦЫ`, `РЕЗУЛЬТАТ`.
- EN text: `SERVICE`, `SKILL`, `SCOPE`, `RESULT`.
- Style continuity: establishes the dark photographic scene, exact `#9CFB51` connector lines, white/lime Bebas Neue, outlined checkpoints, crisp low-key light, and grain.
- Semantic job: explain that «умею пользоваться ИИ» is not yet an offer; the client buys a defined output.

## Frame 2 — КЕЙСЫ / CASES

- Story beat: a portfolio case proves the process by showing the task, one correction, and the checked final result.
- Visual proof: one cinematic portfolio wall/moodboard contains three large photographic stages of the same project, with a visible correction mark and a final checked result.
- Composition: three-stage moodboard
- Text mode: sequence
- RU text: `КЕЙСЫ`, `ЗАДАЧА`, `ПРАВКА`, `ИТОГ`.
- EN text: `CASES`, `TASK`, `REVISION`, `RESULT`.
- Style continuity: same dark photographic materials, flat type, line weight, check-node motif, palette, light, and grain; only this frame uses a card/matrix layout.
- Semantic job: teach what to show in a case instead of presenting unrelated attractive generations.

## Frame 3 — ЗАЯВКА / LEAD

- Story beat: an AI service still needs source materials, scope, and revision terms before the work can start.
- Visual proof: one client inquiry/brief is the central photographic object; lime callout lines connect it to the supplied files, a clearly bounded deliverable preview, and one revision marker.
- Composition: annotated anatomy
- Text mode: annotated-explainer
- RU text: `ЗАЯВКА`, `ИСХОДНИКИ`, `ГРАНИЦЫ`, `ПРАВКИ`.
- EN text: `LEAD`, `INPUTS`, `SCOPE`, `REVISIONS`.
- Style continuity: same flat Bebas Neue, photographic realism, check nodes, thin `#9CFB51` connectors, low-key light, dark palette, and grain; camera changes to a three-quarter close scene.
- Semantic job: show what every direction requires beyond access to a model.

## Frame 4 — ПЛАН / PLAN

- Story beat: the practical start is one service, three honest cases, then one small real inquiry.
- Visual proof: a rising four-node timeline crosses a cinematic work scene and ends at one checked delivery package ready for the client.
- Composition: milestone timeline
- Text mode: sequence
- RU text: `ПЛАН`, `1 УСЛУГА`, `3 КЕЙСА`, `1 ЗАЯВКА`, `ГОТОВО`.
- EN text: `PLAN`, `1 SERVICE`, `3 CASES`, `1 LEAD`, `READY`.
- Style continuity: same palette, photographic materials, flat Bebas Neue, exact `#9CFB51` lines, check-node motif, light, and grain; composition uses a diagonal timeline rather than split screen, moodboard, or anatomy callouts.
- Semantic job: turn a vague wish for additional income into a bounded first portfolio plan.
