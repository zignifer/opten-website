# Visual plan: best-ai-tools-for-work

## Art direction

Dark editorial still-life photography built from real work materials: carbon-black desk, smoked acrylic, paper, brushed metal, glass screens, and thin fiber-optic lines in exact Opten lime `#9CFB51`. Directional side light, restrained haze, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, and generous negative space keep the frames cinematic rather than dashboard-like. All visible type is flat, printed or projected into the photographed scene in Bebas Neue: condensed uppercase display sans-serif, tall narrow letters, clean poster typography. No logos, product UI, score indicators, floating chat bubbles, or decorative software cards.

The repeating motif is one continuous lime signal line that enters from the lower-left and connects the physical evidence in every frame. Line weight, black/graphite palette, lime color, grain, and hard-edged side light remain consistent across the series. Layout, camera angle, and hero object must change from frame to frame.

- Typography treatment: flat printed or projected Bebas Neue lettering integrated into the photographed surface; no extrusion or embossing.
- Card/grid frames: 1

## Cover — a workbench, not a ranking

- Story beat: the useful AI stack is assembled around different jobs rather than one universal winner.
- Visual proof: a wide overhead workbench with four distinct physical tools: marked-up document, source binder with tabs, calendar/notebook, and designer cutting mat with color swatches; a single lime signal line connects them.
- Composition archetype: wide cinematic still life, asymmetric four-zone composition without labels.
- Text mode: none.
- Style continuity: establishes the carbon-black materials, exact `#9CFB51` signal line, side light, and film grain.
- Semantic job: shows multiple work categories sharing one decision system; it cannot be replaced by a generic laptop photo.

## Frame 1 — define the job

- Story beat: selection starts with a deliverable and acceptance test.
- Visual proof: one sharply lit work order clipped to a metal board; a lime route runs from an input folder to one finished artifact and ends at a physical check mark stamp.
- Composition: hero visual, low three-quarter camera.
- Text mode: headline-only
- RU text: `СНАЧАЛА ЗАДАЧА`.
- EN text: `START WITH THE JOB`.
- Style continuity: first style anchor; carbon desk, smoked acrylic, exact lime line, flat Bebas Neue printing, directional side light, grain.
- Semantic job: turns the abstract instruction “define the task” into a visible input-to-deliverable contract.

## Frame 2 — match category to tool

- Story beat: different work categories need different tool surfaces.
- Visual proof: four materially distinct bays on one industrial selection bench: document and pen, cited source pages, calendar/spreadsheet sheet, and design cutting mat.
- Composition: one allowed matrix, 2 x 2 top-down selection table.
- Text mode: annotated-explainer
- RU text: `ТЕКСТ`, `ИСТОЧНИКИ`, `ОФИС`, `ДИЗАЙН`.
- EN text: `WRITING`, `SOURCES`, `OFFICE`, `DESIGN`.
- Style continuity: inherit medium, color grade, lime lines, flat typography, and light from Inline 1; do not copy its layout, camera, clipped work order, or object composition.
- Semantic job: teaches the category map that structures the shortlist, not a decorative list of brand names.

## Frame 3 — run one fair test

- Story beat: compare candidates using the same input, timebox, and review rule.
- Visual proof: one source packet passes through three parallel mechanical test lanes and converges on a single illuminated review loupe.
- Composition: horizontal process timeline, eye-level wide camera.
- Text mode: sequence
- RU text: `ОДНА ЗАДАЧА`, `ТРИ ПРОГОНА`, `ОДНА ПРОВЕРКА`.
- EN text: `ONE TASK`, `THREE RUNS`, `ONE REVIEW`.
- Style continuity: inherit carbon materials, exact lime signal, flat Bebas Neue, side light, and grain; do not reuse the top-down matrix or the hero-board composition.
- Semantic job: makes the bake-off method visible and supports the named practical case in the adjacent step.

## Frame 4 — approve the workflow

- Story beat: a good output is not enough; the workflow also has to fit access, data, cost, and human review.
- Visual proof: a heavy industrial decision gate with four physical inspection checkpoints before a finished folder can leave the workbench.
- Composition: cutaway anatomy / decision gate, centered frontal camera.
- Text mode: annotated-explainer
- RU text: `ДАННЫЕ`, `ДОСТУП`, `ЦЕНА`, `ПРОВЕРКА`.
- EN text: `DATA`, `ACCESS`, `COST`, `REVIEW`.
- Style continuity: same medium, exact lime, line weight, flat type, hard side light, and grain; new frontal gate composition and different hero object.
- Semantic job: shows the four adoption checks that separate a useful demo from a safe repeatable work process.

## Localization and QA

Generate the RU frame first. Create each EN frame as a localization edit of its approved RU pair: change only the words, preserve subject, composition, camera, light, materials, and line placement. Use `seo2/Reference/bebas-neue-font-reference.png` only as the typography reference. Every output is resized and compressed to 1600 x 900 JPG without adding or replacing text in post-processing.
