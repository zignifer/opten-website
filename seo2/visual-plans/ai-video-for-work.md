# Visual production plan — ai-video-for-work

## Article art direction

- Medium: cinematic miniature film studio photographed as a real practical set, combined with restrained flat editorial graphics.
- Materials: smoked glass, brushed black aluminum, matte product surfaces, translucent film strips, optical lenses, and physical storyboard frames.
- Background: deep teal-black `#011417`.
- Accent: exact Opten lime `#9CFB51`, plus a restrained warm red recording light.
- Typography treatment: flat printed Bebas Neue integrated into glass or paper surfaces; never extruded, embossed, beveled, engraved, or rendered as separate 3D letters.
- Line language: one thin `#9CFB51` camera-path line with consistent stroke weight and small practical frame marks.
- Light and grain: low-key studio light, narrow highlights on glass and metal, subtle film grain, controlled reflections.
- Recurring motif: a translucent motion ribbon and one small red recording light appear naturally in every inline frame.
- Card/grid frames: 0
- Old blog usage: inspect only for composition ideas; never attach old JPG files to the generator because their fonts are obsolete.
- Style anchor: Frame 1 RU. Later frames inherit medium, color grade, typography physics, line weight, reflections, and light, but not its layout or camera.
- RU language rule: all explanatory text is Russian. English is allowed only in an official mode name when the Russian explanation is visible beside it.

## Frame 1 — FORMAT / ФОРМАТ

- Story beat: text-to-video and image-to-video start from different inputs and therefore need different instructions.
- Visual proof: one cinematic portal divides into two practical transformations, a written scene becoming a film frame and a product photograph becoming controlled motion.
- Composition: optical diptych
- Text mode: contrast-pair
- RU text: `ФОРМАТ`, `ТЕКСТ → РОЛИК`, `КАДР → ДВИЖЕНИЕ`.
- EN text: `FORMAT`, `TEXT → VIDEO`, `FRAME → MOTION`.
- Style continuity: smoked glass, black aluminum, flat Bebas Neue, lime path line, motion ribbon, red recording light, same low-key grade.
- Semantic job: make the mode choice visible instead of presenting two disconnected labels.

## Frame 2 — CAMERA / КАМЕРА

- Story beat: one precise camera path turns a vague product idea into a usable shot.
- Visual proof: a matte cosmetics bottle stands on a miniature set while a real camera carriage follows one restrained lime path toward it.
- Composition: cinematic hero visual
- Text mode: headline-only
- RU text: `КАМЕРА`.
- EN text: `CAMERA`.
- Style continuity: identical materials, grade, flat type, lime line weight, motion ribbon, red recording light, and controlled reflections.
- Semantic job: the physical camera rail proves the value of one explicit movement without filling the frame with vocabulary.

## Frame 3 — MOTION / ДВИЖЕНИЕ

- Story beat: a video prompt becomes controllable when motion is written as a short causal sequence.
- Visual proof: three translucent exposure frames show the same subject at the start, during one action, and in the final position along a single motion ribbon.
- Composition: curved film sequence
- Text mode: sequence
- RU text: `ДВИЖЕНИЕ`, `СТАРТ → ДЕЙСТВИЕ → ФИНАЛ`.
- EN text: `MOTION`, `START → ACTION → END`.
- Style continuity: same smoked glass, black metal, flat type, lime path, motion ribbon, red recording light, and film grain.
- Semantic job: show temporal order through one subject rather than list six prompt components on separate cards.

## Frame 4 — REVIEW / ПРОВЕРКА

- Story beat: the most dramatic clip is not automatically the frame that should enter the edit.
- Visual proof: an optical inspection lens isolates one stable product frame while distorted rejected frames fall into red shadow outside the edit gate.
- Composition: editorial decision point
- Text mode: contrast-pair
- RU text: `ПРОВЕРКА`, `БРАК`, `В МОНТАЖ`.
- EN text: `REVIEW`, `REJECT`, `TO EDIT`.
- Style continuity: same materials, low-key light, lime line, red recording light, motion ribbon, flat Bebas Neue, and controlled grain.
- Semantic job: turn quality control into a visible selection decision instead of a generic checklist.
