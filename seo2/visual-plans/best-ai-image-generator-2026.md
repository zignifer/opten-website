# Visual plan: best-ai-image-generator-2026

## Art direction

Dark editorial product-lab photography: a carbon-black optical bench, smoked glass, brushed graphite, translucent resin samples, precision clamps, photographic test charts, and one continuous signal line in exact Opten lime `#9CFB51`. Hard side light cuts through restrained haze; surfaces carry subtle film grain and real studio wear. Every frame feels like a physical image-testing lab, not a software dashboard or a grid of app logos.

All visible type is flat, printed or projected into the photographed scene in Bebas Neue: condensed uppercase display sans-serif, tall narrow letters, clean poster typography. No extruded or embossed letters, no brand logos, no model UI, no Opten score, no browser chrome, no fake comparison screenshots, and no “before / after” layout.

The repeating motif is a thin lime optical beam that enters from the lower-left, passes through the current test object, and exits toward the upper-right. Carbon materials, exact lime, beam weight, grain, flat type, and hard-edged light stay constant while the camera, hero object, and composition change.

- Typography treatment: flat Bebas Neue lettering printed on paper, glass, or a physical lab label; no 3D text.
- Card/grid frames: 1

- Story beat: there is no universal winner; the best image model depends on the output job.
- Visual proof: one neutral white product object sits at the center of a wide optical bench while six distinct material paths fan outward toward a photo plate, edit mask, reference stack, typography sheet, vector stencil, and art-direction swatch.
- Composition archetype: wide cinematic radial still life, low overhead camera, asymmetric negative space.
- Text mode: none.
- Style continuity: establishes the carbon bench, exact `#9CFB51` optical beam, smoked glass, side light, and grain.
- Semantic job: shows one source object routed into six different production tasks without ranking cards or logos.
