# Visual production plan — where-to-find-ai-clients

## Article art direction

- Medium: cinematic editorial photography of a portable client-acquisition field kit staged as a physical exhibition, with real paper, translucent acrylic, metal clips, printed proofs, and wayfinding objects.
- Materials: matte black hard case, graphite paper, smoked acrylic, folded portfolio sheets, stamped envelopes, proof prints, brass fasteners, and one compact delivery box.
- Background: deep teal-black `#011417` studio cyclorama, never a desk, laptop dashboard, floating UI board, or abstract connector network.
- Accent: exact Opten lime `#9CFB51`, plus white, graphite, and small brushed-metal highlights.
- Typography treatment: flat generated Bebas Neue, condensed uppercase display sans-serif with tall narrow letters; text is printed on paper or acrylic, never extruded, embossed, beveled, engraved, or rendered as separate 3D letters.
- Line language: sparse `#9CFB51` crop marks, stamps, underlines, and short directional arrows with one consistent stroke weight; no web of connectors.
- Light and grain: hard side light like an editorial product shoot, deep controlled shadows, crisp paper texture, restrained reflections, and subtle 35 mm grain.
- Recurring motif: one physical lime approval stamp appears once in every frame and moves from offer to proof to lead to paid plan.
- Information density: one large hero label and 3-4 short connected labels. Every label names a step, source, or decision visible in the physical scene.
- Card/grid frames: 1
- Card/grid note: Frame 2 is the only triptych/card-matrix composition.
- Style anchor: Frame 1 RU. Later frames inherit the physical materials, palette, flat Bebas Neue printing, stamp motif, crop marks, hard side light, and grain, but not its layout, camera, or object arrangement.
- Old blog usage: older reference JPGs are inspected only for information density and visual teaching. They are not attached to generation. Every generation attaches only `seo2/Reference/bebas-neue-font-reference.png`.
- RU language rule: all visible explanatory text is Russian. English appears only in the localized EN sibling.

## Cover — client acquisition field kit

- Story beat: finding work becomes manageable when one clear service, portfolio proof, a client brief, and a delivery plan fit into the same system.
- Visual proof: an open matte-black hard case on a dark studio plinth holds distinct unbranded artifacts: blank offer cards, portfolio proof sheets, a client brief envelope, invoice stub, approval stamp, and delivery checklist.
- Composition archetype: open-case hero still life.
- Text mode: none.
- Semantic job: show the complete client-acquisition system without readable words, logos, money piles, course hype, laptop dashboards, or floating interface panels.

## Frame 1 — УСЛУГА / SERVICE

- Story beat: a broad AI skill becomes buyable only after it is shaped into a bounded service with a concrete delivery.
- Visual proof: an exploded physical service kit transforms loose creative tools into one sealed delivery box; three annotated components define the transformation.
- Composition: exploded-object anatomy
- Text mode: annotated-explainer
- RU text: `УСЛУГА`, `ЗАДАЧА`, `ГРАНИЦЫ`, `РЕЗУЛЬТАТ`.
- EN text: `SERVICE`, `JOB`, `SCOPE`, `RESULT`.
- Style continuity: establishes the dark exhibition set, exact `#9CFB51`, flat white/lime Bebas Neue printing, crop marks, approval stamp, hard side light, and 35 mm grain.
- Semantic job: teach why «умею пользоваться ИИ» is not an offer until the output and boundaries are visible.

## Frame 2 — КЕЙСЫ / CASES

- Story beat: a portfolio case earns trust by showing the task, one corrected mistake, and the manually reviewed result.
- Visual proof: one freestanding accordion portfolio opens into three photographic proof panels of the same product-card project; the middle panel carries a visible correction mark and the last carries the lime approval stamp.
- Composition: accordion triptych proof
- Text mode: sequence
- RU text: `КЕЙСЫ`, `ЗАДАЧА`, `ПРАВКА`, `ИТОГ`.
- EN text: `CASES`, `TASK`, `REVISION`, `RESULT`.
- Style continuity: same paper/acrylic materials, flat Bebas Neue, palette, stamp, crop marks, hard light, and grain; this is the only card/matrix frame.
- Semantic job: show what a credible practice case contains instead of presenting unrelated attractive generations.

## Frame 3 — ЗАЯВКА / LEAD

- Story beat: early work comes from a few chosen channels, but every contact needs a relevant case and a clear brief.
- Visual proof: a creator stands at a cinematic four-way exhibition junction carrying one case folio and one brief envelope; four physical signposts name the channels without using floating panels or a connector web.
- Composition: cinematic decision junction
- Text mode: annotated-explainer
- RU text: `ЗАЯВКА`, `ЗНАКОМЫЕ`, `БИЗНЕС`, `СООБЩЕСТВО`, `ПАРТНЕРЫ`.
- EN text: `LEAD`, `CONTACTS`, `BUSINESS`, `COMMUNITY`, `PARTNERS`.
- Style continuity: same physical printing, exact lime, approval stamp, crop-mark geometry, hard side light, and grain; camera changes to a wide human-scale scene.
- Semantic job: explain where to look for first tasks while showing that a case and brief travel with the outreach.

## Frame 4 — ПЛАН / PLAN

- Story beat: a short relevant message should lead to a small paid stage, reviewed delivery, and permission to publish the case.
- Visual proof: a low-angle runway of four physical milestones ends at a checked delivery box; an outreach envelope moves through the sequence and receives the recurring approval stamp at the end.
- Composition: milestone runway
- Text mode: sequence
- RU text: `ПЛАН`, `СООБЩЕНИЕ`, `ТЕСТ`, `ПРОВЕРКА`, `КЕЙС`.
- EN text: `PLAN`, `MESSAGE`, `PAID TEST`, `REVIEW`, `CASE`.
- Style continuity: same materials, flat Bebas Neue printing, exact `#9CFB51`, stamp, crop marks, hard side light, and grain; composition is a deep perspective path rather than anatomy, triptych, or junction.
- Semantic job: turn vague outreach into a bounded path from first contact to reusable proof.
