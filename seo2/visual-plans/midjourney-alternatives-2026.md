# Visual production sheet: midjourney-alternatives-2026

## Series system

- Art direction: an analog optical darkroom for choosing an image model, built from translucent film, glass prisms, contact sheets, precision rulers, and real printed outputs rather than floating software panels.
- Medium/materials: cinematic product photography with smoked acrylic, frosted glass, black anodized metal, photographic film, subtle dust, and fine paper grain.
- Background: deep teal-black `#011417`, almost black at the edges, with a restrained soft cyan fill and a narrow exact `#9CFB51` laser accent.
- Lighting: low-key practical darkroom light, hard-edged reflections on glass, shallow haze, and controlled pools of light around the proof object.
- Palette: `#011417`, exact `#9CFB51`, off-white paper, muted cyan, and small neutral silver details. No yellow-green substitution.
- Line language: thin flat `#9CFB51` registration lines, crop marks, arrows, and film perforation ticks. No glowing wireframe network.
- Typography treatment: flat Bebas Neue, condensed uppercase display sans-serif, tall narrow letters, clean poster typography; printed on physical film or paper inside the generated raster, never extruded, embossed, metallic, or added in post.
- Repeating motif: a single beam enters the frame, passes through one physical decision object, and exits as a controlled set of proof marks or outputs.
- Card/grid frames: 0
- Humanized frames: 1
- Style continuity: every frame keeps the darkroom materials, exact lime registration lines, cool glass reflections, film grain, and flat Bebas Neue treatment while changing the camera, object, and composition archetype.
