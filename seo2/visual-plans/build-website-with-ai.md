# Visual production plan — build-website-with-ai

## Article art direction

- Medium: cinematic product-design print workshop photographed as a real practical set, with a landing page represented by folded paper, translucent vellum, metal crop frames, and physical proofing tools rather than fake software UI.
- Materials: charcoal paper, milky vellum, matte black aluminum, smoked glass, pale concrete, steel clips, and one translucent lime acrylic ruler.
- Background: deep teal-black `#011417`.
- Accent: exact Opten lime `#9CFB51`, restrained warm white paper, and small neutral steel highlights.
- Typography treatment: flat printed Bebas Neue on paper tabs, proof strips, or vellum; never extruded, embossed, beveled, engraved, backlit as dimensional lettering, or built as separate 3D letters.
- Line language: thin `#9CFB51` crop marks, registration crosses, and proofing arrows with one consistent stroke weight.
- Light and grain: directional print-room light, a narrow softbox reflection on glass, deep controlled shadows, tactile paper fibers, and subtle analog grain.
- Recurring motif: a translucent lime crop ruler and two steel binder clips appear naturally in every inline frame.
- Card/grid frames: 0
- Batch separation: no laptop dashboard, floating UI board, dark desk, connector network, architectural rail, steel registration pins, or acrylic page-construction rig from the previous article.
- Style anchor: Frame 1 RU. Later RU frames inherit the material system, color grade, flat typography, crop-line weight, binder clips, and light, but must not copy its layout, camera, or object arrangement.
- RU language rule: all explanatory text is Cyrillic except the official term `CTA`, which is retained because it is used in the article and interface work.

## Cover — LANDING PAGE PROOF PRESS

- Story beat: AI can accelerate a landing-page draft, but the useful result comes from a brief, deliberate structure, and human review.
- Visual proof: one long folded paper landing page passes through a compact matte-black proof press while a translucent lime ruler aligns its sections; nearby vellum thumbnails, color swatches, and a steel inspection loupe show preparation and review.
- Composition: cinematic three-quarter hero object with crop-safe negative space.
- Text mode: none.
- Text: no text, no logos, no readable interface.
- Style continuity: same print-workshop materials, deep teal-black background, exact lime ruler, crop marks, binder clips, directional light, and analog grain as the inline series.
- Semantic job: establish the build-and-proof workflow without a generic computer screen or fake app interface.

## Frame 1 — TASK / ЗАДАЧА

- Story beat: before generation, define the business task that the page must solve.
- Visual proof: an opened charcoal brief folder is seen from above; four physical vellum tabs converge on one small landing-page proof in the center.
- Composition: radial anatomy flat-lay
- Text mode: annotated-explainer
- RU text: `ЗАДАЧА`, `ЦЕЛЬ`, `АУДИТОРИЯ`, `ОФФЕР`, `ФАКТЫ`.
- EN text: `TASK`, `GOAL`, `AUDIENCE`, `OFFER`, `FACTS`.
- Style continuity: paper, vellum, exact lime crop ruler and lines, steel clips, flat Bebas Neue, directional light, and grain.
- Semantic job: show the four inputs that prevent a generic AI website draft.

## Frame 2 — LAYOUT / МАКЕТ

- Story beat: a useful layout turns those inputs into a page order and a clear action, including the mobile version.
- Visual proof: a vague blank accordion fold on the left becomes a deliberate long landing-page proof on the right, with the same sections also nested into a narrow phone-sized crop frame.
- Composition: horizontal before-and-after transformation
- Text mode: sequence
- RU text: `МАКЕТ`, `СМЫСЛ → БЛОКИ → CTA → МОБИЛЬНАЯ`.
- EN text: `LAYOUT`, `PURPOSE → SECTIONS → CTA → MOBILE`.
- Style continuity: same materials, exact lime marks, binder clips, flat type, light, and grain, with a low oblique camera instead of Frame 1's top-down view.
- Semantic job: prove that layout is information hierarchy and responsive behavior, not decoration.

## Frame 3 — PROMPT

- Story beat: the prompt should carry context and limits into a concrete landing-page draft.
- Visual proof: one printed prompt sheet moves through three mechanical proofing stations; each station adds a visible layer to the same folded page until a coherent draft emerges.
- Composition: diagonal production sequence
- Text mode: sequence
- RU text: `ПРОМПТ`, `КОНТЕКСТ → ОГРАНИЧЕНИЯ → РЕЗУЛЬТАТ`.
- EN text: `PROMPT`, `CONTEXT → CONSTRAINTS → RESULT`.
- Style continuity: same paper and vellum, lime crop ruler, line weight, clips, flat Bebas Neue, light, and grain, without the radial or before-and-after layout.
- Semantic job: show why a detailed instruction produces an inspectable result while a wish for a "modern site" does not.

## Frame 4 — PROJECT / ПРОЕКТ

- Story beat: the draft becomes a project only after mobile, copy and action, speed, and form behavior are checked.
- Visual proof: a finished folded landing-page proof sits under a large inspection loupe while four physical check tabs lock around it; one red rejected strip stays outside the final project sleeve.
- Composition: asymmetric inspection decision point
- Text mode: annotated-explainer
- RU text: `ПРОЕКТ`, `МОБИЛЬНАЯ`, `ТЕКСТ + CTA`, `СКОРОСТЬ`, `ФОРМА`.
- EN text: `PROJECT`, `MOBILE`, `COPY + CTA`, `SPEED`, `FORM`.
- Style continuity: same material system, exact lime, crop marks, clips, flat type, light, and grain, with a tight inspection camera and no sequence layout.
- Semantic job: make human QA and the publish/no-publish decision visible as the boundary between a draft and a sellable landing page.
