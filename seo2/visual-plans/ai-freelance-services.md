# Visual production plan — ai-freelance-services

## Article art direction

- Medium: cinematic editorial photography of a real freelance fulfillment bench where hands sort, proof, package, and hand off small client deliverables; the scene must feel worked in, not arranged as a generic office still life or rendered as an interface.
- Article-native practical set: a compact studio dispatch table with a presentation booklet, product-card print, narrow landing-page proof, short-video contact sheet, client request, invoice stub, and delivery sleeve. Human hands perform one clear action in every frame.
- Materials: dark teal painted steel, warm uncoated stock, smoked glass, black cotton portfolio sleeves, brushed nickel clips, and one exact-lime woven job ticket.
- Background: deep teal-black `#011417`.
- Accent: exact Opten lime `#9CFB51`; a muted warm white for paper and one restrained brick-red mark only on rejected or unfocused work.
- Typography treatment: flat Bebas Neue printed directly on job tickets, proof strips, or the tabletop graphic layer; never extruded, embossed, beveled, engraved, backlit, or built as separate 3D letters.
- Line language: thin `#9CFB51` handoff arrows and crop marks with a single consistent stroke weight; lines connect an action or decision and never form a dashboard network.
- Light and grain: low-key side light from a large soft source, narrow controlled highlights on metal, deep but readable shadows, tactile fibers, natural hand texture, and subtle editorial grain.
- Recurring motif: one woven `#9CFB51` job ticket and a small black spring clip appear naturally in each inline frame.
- Photo-first hierarchy: the hands, finished deliverable, and visible decision remain clear when the text is mentally removed; typography and arrows only name the action already proven by the scene.
- Card/grid frames: 0
- Batch separation: no laptop dashboard, floating UI board, connector network, architectural rail, print press, translucent crop ruler, steel registration pins, suspended cards, acrylic modules, or conveyor rig.
- Style anchor: Frame 1 RU. Later RU frames inherit its practical-set realism, color grade, flat type, lime ticket, line weight, low-key light, and grain, but must not copy its camera, layout, or object arrangement.
- RU language rule: all explanatory text is Cyrillic.

## Cover — FREELANCE DELIVERY BENCH

- Story beat: work with AI becomes sellable only when a broad skill is turned into a bounded deliverable that can be reviewed and handed to a client.
- Visual proof: two hands arrange one presentation booklet, one product-card proof, one narrow landing-page strip, a client request, an invoice stub, and a checked delivery sleeve into a clear handoff set.
- Composition: cinematic three-quarter hero action with the hands and final sleeve dominant, plus crop-safe negative space.
- Text mode: none.
- Text: no text, no logos, no readable interface.
- Style continuity: same dark teal steel, warm paper, black sleeves, exact-lime woven ticket, controlled side light, tactile materials, and grain as the inline series.
- Semantic job: establish the world of small, concrete freelance deliverables without promising income or showing a generic computer screen.

## Frame 1 — SERVICE / УСЛУГА

- Story beat: a beginner should sell one concrete result, not a vague ability to use AI.
- Visual proof: a hand pulls one finished presentation booklet from a mixed pile of raw text sheets, image proofs, and video contact strips, then slides it into a labeled delivery sleeve.
- Cinematic proof: a close oblique photograph shows real fingers, paper edges, cloth texture, and directional light; the action is readable before the editorial type.
- Composition: diagonal handoff transformation
- Text mode: contrast-pair
- RU text: `УСЛУГА`, `НАВЫК → РЕЗУЛЬТАТ`.
- EN text: `SERVICE`, `SKILL → RESULT`.
- Style continuity: establishes the dark steel bench, warm stock, black sleeve, exact-lime woven ticket, flat Bebas Neue, thin arrow, side light, and grain.
- Semantic job: make the difference between knowing a tool and delivering a client-ready outcome visible.

## Frame 2 — CASES / КЕЙСЫ

- Story beat: three self-assigned portfolio cases prove one service better than a list of tools.
- Visual proof: three different finished artifacts for the same fictional neighborhood business are fanned in depth under one inspection light: a presentation booklet, a product-card proof, and a narrow landing-page strip, each held by a small clip.
- Cinematic proof: the frame reads as a physical portfolio review with shallow depth, tactile print surfaces, hand-marked revisions, and a real inspection gesture rather than a grid of cards.
- Composition: fan-shaped portfolio inspection
- Text mode: annotated-explainer
- RU text: `КЕЙСЫ`, `ПРЕЗЕНТАЦИЯ`, `КАРТОЧКА`, `ЛЕНДИНГ`.
- EN text: `CASES`, `PRESENTATION`, `PRODUCT CARD`, `LANDING PAGE`.
- Style continuity: same bench, materials, exact-lime ticket, flat type, line weight, side light, and grain, with a wider top-oblique camera and no diagonal handoff layout.
- Semantic job: show what a compact three-case portfolio contains and why outputs matter more than tool names.

## Frame 3 — LEAD / ЗАЯВКА

- Story beat: choose the first service by matching one real client problem to one result you can finish and check.
- Visual proof: a client request sheet with one problem line highlighted sits at the center; a hand moves one presentation booklet token toward it while six mismatched sample artifacts remain outside the lit area.
- Cinematic proof: a tight decision-point photograph uses hand movement, selective light, and physical distance between the chosen deliverable and rejected options; no fake application screen is needed.
- Composition: asymmetric decision point
- Text mode: contrast-pair
- RU text: `ЗАЯВКА`, `ЗАДАЧА → ОДНА УСЛУГА`.
- EN text: `LEAD`, `TASK → ONE SERVICE`.
- Style continuity: same tactile materials, exact-lime ticket and arrow, flat Bebas Neue, controlled side light, and grain, with a tight near-eye-level crop rather than the earlier oblique views.
- Semantic job: teach the selection rule: start from a bounded client problem, not the longest possible service menu.

## Frame 4 — PLAN / ПЛАН

- Story beat: build three portfolio cases as a repeatable sequence of brief, revision, and checked result.
- Visual proof: one deliverable travels through three real work zones in depth: clipped request at the front, marked draft in the middle, and finished sleeved result held up at the back; the same lime ticket advances with it.
- Cinematic proof: a low longitudinal camera captures real depth, shadows, paper wear, and a final handoff gesture; the sequence is physical and photographed rather than drawn as a timeline slide.
- Composition: deep longitudinal production sequence
- Text mode: sequence
- RU text: `ПЛАН`, `ЗАДАЧА → ПРАВКА → РЕЗУЛЬТАТ`.
- EN text: `PLAN`, `TASK → REVISION → RESULT`.
- Style continuity: same bench, black sleeve, exact-lime ticket, flat type, arrow weight, low-key light, and grain, with a long-depth camera unlike the previous three compositions.
- Semantic job: make the portfolio plan repeatable and reviewable instead of presenting three decorative mockups.
