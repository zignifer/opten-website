# Visual production plan — ai-text-for-work

## Article art direction

- Medium: cinematic editorial photography combined with restrained flat poster graphics.
- Materials: real paper, ink, red editor pencil, black metal frames, glass, and printed proofs.
- Background: deep teal-black `#011417`.
- Accent: exact Opten lime `#9CFB51`.
- Typography treatment: flat printed Bebas Neue integrated into the image plane; never extruded, embossed, beveled, engraved, or rendered as separate 3D letters.
- Line language: thin `#9CFB51` rules and crop marks with one consistent stroke weight.
- Light and grain: controlled editorial studio light, soft paper shadows, restrained film grain, neutral off-white highlights.
- Recurring motif: one red editor pencil and one thin lime proofing line appear naturally across the series.
- Card/grid frames: 0
- Old blog usage: inspect only for composition ideas; never attach old JPG files to the generator because their fonts are obsolete.
- Style anchor: Frame 1 RU. Later frames inherit medium, grade, typography physics, line weight, and light, but not its layout or camera.

## Frame 1 — TASK / ЗАДАЧА

- Story beat: the same source facts become different copy when the job changes.
- Visual proof: one central fact sheet physically splits into two finished outputs, an email on the left and a landing hero on the right.
- Composition: asymmetric split-screen
- Text mode: contrast-pair
- RU text: `ЗАДАЧА`, `ПИСЬМО`, `ЛЕНДИНГ`.
- EN text: `TASK`, `EMAIL`, `LANDING PAGE`.
- Style continuity: flat Bebas Neue, black paper frames, lime crop marks, red pencil, identical editorial grade.
- Semantic job: show why naming the document and its function changes the generated text.

## Frame 2 — STRUCTURE / СТРУКТУРА

- Story beat: a prompt needs a connected causal spine, not a pile of labels.
- Visual proof: three transparent editorial layers align over one draft: reader silhouette, target mark, and final page shape.
- Composition: layered anatomy
- Text mode: sequence
- RU text: `СТРУКТУРА`, `КТО → ЗАЧЕМ → ЧТО`.
- EN text: `STRUCTURE`, `WHO → WHY → WHAT`.
- Style continuity: same flat type, teal-black grade, lime proofing line, red pencil, paper and glass materials.
- Semantic job: turn role, goal, and output into one visible sequence instead of disconnected noun cards.

## Frame 3 — PROMPT

- Story beat: “write a Telegram post” becomes useful when it centers on one concrete work situation.
- Visual proof: a pile of repetitive generic draft strips falls out of focus while one sharp scene card shows a promise trapped in chat and missing from the task plan.
- Composition: cinematic focus shift
- Text mode: contrast-pair
- RU text: `PROMPT`, `ОБЩИЕ СЛОВА`, `ОДНА СИТУАЦИЯ`.
- EN text: `PROMPT`, `GENERIC COPY`, `ONE SITUATION`.
- Style continuity: same flat type and lime rules, paper artifacts, red pencil, editorial lighting and grain.
- Semantic job: visualize the article's named ChatGPT case rather than enumerate prompt components.

## Frame 4 — RESULT / РЕЗУЛЬТАТ

- Story beat: publication happens after a human editorial decision.
- Visual proof: one final page under a magnifying proofing lens, red corrections outside the clean area, and a single lime approval stroke.
- Composition: hero visual
- Text mode: headline-only
- RU text: `РЕЗУЛЬТАТ`.
- EN text: `RESULT`.
- Style continuity: same flat Bebas Neue, teal-black grade, lime proof line, red pencil, off-white paper, and restrained grain.
- Semantic job: make editorial verification the protagonist without filling the frame with checklist words.
